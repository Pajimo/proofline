# Work Proofline will not document

Proofline drafts job reports and invoices. It never produces compliance certificates:

- **Gas Safe records** (including CP12 landlord gas safety records). Gas work must be done and certified by a Gas Safe registered engineer.
- **Electrical certificates** (EIC, Minor Works, EICR). These need a qualified, registered electrician.

The job pack's `excluded` list (see `packs/plumbing.yaml`) holds keywords for this work. If a job appears to include it, Proofline blocks those lines and tells the tradesperson. It does not draft them.
